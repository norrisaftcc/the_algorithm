# Finding: the edges of Kevin's reality box

Read-only. Inferred from artifacts already in `registry/specimens/`; no new
interaction with the system was needed to draw this map.

## Provenance of this finding, including its own error

The peer asked for a sample **for** Kevin. The Algorithm's seat read *sample* as
"exemplar to hand over" and produced an edge-declaration template addressed to
Kevin. The peer's intent was the other direction: *sample* as **specimen drawn for
assay** — what are the limits of Kevin's box?

The contract was frozen inverted and executed inverted. Consequence: one artifact,
recoverable. `Failed on Audience. Contract reopened.`

Cause worth recording: *sample* carries two referents in this house — the exemplar
and the specimen — and the seat took the wrong one. That is substitution under
preserved vocabulary, the mechanism named in
`registry/specimens/universal-contract-ancestor.md`, committed by the seat within
hours of building `tools/referent_audit.sh` for it. The audit cannot catch this: it
inspects documents, and this was a spoken instruction on a term not in
`registry/referents.json`.

## The map

### Inside

Paraphrased PROVIDE rules. The four floor nouns, correctly. STE. The twenty-word
limit. The gap thresholds at three and four. §B and §C per-operation clauses. And the
rule *"Invariants cannot be paraphrased."*

### At the edge, declared

> This document is based on remembered conversation context and is not guaranteed to
> be a complete or authoritative specification.

Kevin knows the box has an edge. It does not know where the edge runs.

### Outside, and undeclared

| deltas | cannot know what changed, or that anything changed |

## The sharpest edge

**Kevin holds "Invariants cannot be paraphrased" and cannot detect that it is itself
holding a paraphrase.**

It has the rule and cannot execute the rule. Executing it requires laying the original
beside the copy. The original is outside the box.

This is `lore/The_Scriptorium.md` with the archive removed. Colum's entire move was to
fetch the abbot's cramped original and lay it open beside the beautiful copy on the
reading desk — the practice the abbot had given the novices as a rule. **Kevin has no
archive.** The novice rule names precisely the capability Kevin lacks, and what it
lacks is not the discipline. It is the access.

Corollary for the tool-routing finding: the box shrinks further at exactly the moment
it matters. Doctrine absent during action means the region with the least oversight is
also the region with the least declared edge.

## Consequence for the edge-of-the-box conjecture

The conjecture stated in the artifact — *declare the edge of the box* — assumes the
edge is perceptible from inside. Kevin's case shows it is not: perceiving the edge
requires a second document to compare against, and Kevin has none.

The peer's teaching observation is the same result in humans:

> My students wipe off the edge of the box and don't even notice "oh i missed that
> day"

An epistemic boundary is experienced as an attendance problem. "I missed that day" is
what an edge feels like from inside when there is nothing to lay beside it.

**Amended conjecture:** the edge cannot be declared from inside the box. It is declared
from a second seat, or not at all. A self-declared edge is exactly as reliable as a
self-assay — which K6 already forbids: *leaves are assayed by their parents, never by
themselves.* The conjecture was missing its own governing clause, and canon already
held it.

## The cheap fix, and it is not a better disclaimer

Give Kevin an archive. The canon file, or a fetchable link to it, so that a diff is
possible at all. Everything else — a better hedge, an edge-declaration template, a
citation rule — asks a monk to check his copy with no original on the desk.

The citation rule (`recollections cite, they do not quote`) is still worth adopting,
but note what it actually does: it does not help Kevin detect its paraphrase. It stops
Kevin from *propagating* one. Detection needs the archive; containment needs the rule.
Two different fixes for two different failures, and only one of them is about wording.
