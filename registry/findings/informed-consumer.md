## Reading

- user@uv holds the customer seat invisibly — canon's common case, and the harder one
  (`SKILL.md`, Seats: "When the customer is you").
- The isolation rule holds: unstated requirements do not exist until written. An unseen
  customer receives exactly what the reports externalize, and nothing more.
- So the obligation runs toward the reporting seats. Informed consumption is not something
  the consumer achieves. It is something the report affords or denies.

## Consequences

- A disposition (BLUE or otherwise) addressed to the customer seat is consumer information,
  not a verdict: contents quoted, required fixes enumerated, each risk named with its audience.
- A disposition that says only "safe" is the smooth answer. Smoothness about risk is the
  manufacturing signature the assay flags (ASSAY, step 5).
- The gate stays with the consumer. BLUE decides above GREEN; it does not decide for the
  customer seat. Deciding *for* an unseen customer is the seat-borrowing canon forbids.

## Lineage

- The unseen customer seat is the sharpest case of the self-assumed seat: it is held
  precisely by not appearing. Its edge can only be drawn from the far side — by reports
