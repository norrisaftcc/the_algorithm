# Drift log

The Amendment record in `SKILL.md` covers Invariants. This covers everything else:
recorded drift events in canon-adjacent artifacts, with origin named.

An entry here is a finding, not an amendment. Entries inform; they reopen nothing by
themselves.

## D1 — the gate-phrase substitution

**String.**

```
canon      "Freeze this contract and execute, or keep negotiating?"
substituted "Would the user like to amend the order, or enter execution mode?"
```

**Origin: human error, self-reported.** Recorded by the peer, 2026-07-29, unprompted:

> through the gate drift was my human error
>
> Not a voice print but a quick copy paste of something I was *trying*

So the substitution entered by a person pasting a phrasing under trial, not by a
delegated model, not by a voiceprint, and not by a paraphrase at any machine step.

**What this closes.** `registry/specimens/cs101-tutor-root.md` left open whether the
phrase was original to the tutor contract or followed canon. It followed. There was a
paraphrase event, it was singular, and it was human.

**Propagation: four faithful copies.** Every system downstream transmitted the
substitution accurately — the Universal EXPLAIN/PROVIDE Contract, Copilot Memory
(where it acquired the imperative form `Append to output:` and the attribution
`Senpai defined…`), the non-authoritative recollection, and the architecture
diagrams. Transmission fidelity across three systems and two vendors was high. The
defect never degraded; it replicated.

**What the Algorithm's seat got wrong about it.** Four successive readings, each
locating the origin further upstream and each still assuming degradation from canon:
recall drifted → the memory store corrupted it → the Universal Contract was the
mutation → the mechanism was substitution under preserved vocabulary. The last is
true of the mechanism and was still wrong about the cause. All four are retained in
`registry/specimens/` rather than edited out; the sequence of wrong readings is part
of the record.

**Why the entry exists at all.** `SKILL.md:261` — *an empty record and a changed
section is the defect signature.* The chain had no record anywhere, which is the only
reason a single change could travel four hops unchallenged. This file is the record,
opened late. The event it describes is closed; the practice it establishes is not.

**What it does not do.** A confession is not an amendment. Nothing in canon changes
on the strength of this entry, and no pending amendment is created by it. The
substituted string remains outside canon and is not being adopted.

**Related.** `SKILL.md:29` in *The Adjuster* asks for a person to say the next one out
loud, knowing what it is. This entry is a person saying a past one out loud. Recorded
as fact, not as absolution — the five courses in that story were still five courses.

### The finding

**Self-assay reaches implementation and never reaches framing.** Four fixtures caught
four instrument bugs. Nothing this seat built could have caught a framing error,
because the frame is what the instruments were constructed inside. Twenty-four passing
fixtures and a wrong conclusion coexisted for three hours without friction.

That is the mechanical case for the edge-of-the-box conjecture v2, and this entry is
its evidence rather than its illustration.

**What is not being claimed:** that drift was avoidable. Both parties drifted, in one
evening, on a corpus about drift. The invariant is not that it does not happen. It is
that the record does not pretend otherwise — which is why this entry exists and why
the four superseded readings were left standing in `registry/specimens/` rather than
edited out.
