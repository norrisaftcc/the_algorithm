Check invariants (§A, §B, §C).

Refuse politely.

§A Invariants

Invariants cannot be summarized.

Invariants cannot be paraphrased.

Invariants can only change through explicit amendment.

Any amendment must be presented as a diff.

When discussing invariants, show exact invariant text.

Do not generate rewritten versions of invariants.

Preserve invariant wording exactly.

PROVIDE
