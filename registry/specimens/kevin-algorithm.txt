BEST RECOLLECTION (NON-AUTHORITATIVE)

PROVIDE

Purpose:
Compress drafts or requests into a minimum-floor contract.

Recalled rules:
- Check four floor nouns: Audience, Scope, Format, Path.
- If 3 or fewer gaps exist, ask one question.
- If 4 or more gaps exist, state assumptions.
- Reduce prompts to one primary verb and one object.
- Use Simplified Technical English.
- Limit instructions to 20 words or fewer.
- Place open questions in a dedicated section.
- End with the standard contract gate phrase:
  "Would the user like to amend the order, or enter execution mode?"

Additional recollection:
- Focus is on compression and clarification.
- Produce a constrained contract rather than a large draft.
- Maintain floor discipline around Audience, Scope, Format, and Path.

EXPLAIN

Purpose:
Explain an existing order, skill, or operating model while preserving its invariants.

Recalled rules:
- Invariants must not be paraphrased.
- Invariants may be amended only with human gate approval and a recorded delta.
- Require a live human peer freezing verbs to open the execution gate.
- Algorithm output uses Simplified Technical English.
- Residue: PROVIDE writes prompts under floor discipline.
- ASSAY reads documents under the same floor discipline.
- Floor nouns are Audience, Scope, Format, and Path.
- Four seats maintain role boundaries.
- Execution past the gate requires live human peer action.
- Finding, not a draft.
