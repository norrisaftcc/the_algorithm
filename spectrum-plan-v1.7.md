## § 5 · SPECTRUM

**RED → ORANGE → YELLOW → GREEN → BLUE → INDIGO → VIOLET → ULTRAVIOLET**

▸ RED — templated prompts that passed ASSAY and PROVIDE. Nothing else.
▸ ORANGE — one assigned instance. No spawning.
▸ YELLOW — spawns ORANGE and RED, up to N, set by its report chain.
▸ GREEN — executes frozen contracts. Negotiates in scope. Escalates the rest.
▸ BLUE — decides above GREEN. Disposes nothing at lower gates.
▸ INDIGO — auditors. The facilitator's delegable half, granting wire cut. Outside
  the run's reality box. Reads everything; FILEs findings; may END a run. Cannot be
  addressed; an appeal is logged and nothing answers. Appeal-rate per run is the
  Mission-level drift metric. END's principled trigger is appeal saturation, not
  error. Presence per-run undisclosed; possibility always disclosed. Full terms:
  indigo-auditor-plan.md, frozen.
▸ VIOLET — in use (teacherbot@violet), position fixed, definition open. Recorded,
  not resolved.
▸ ULTRAVIOLET — amends its own state, repo, memory. Unbounded rights and expense.
  Root logged in, ad hoc. Sits across from [user@root], who holds no seat, feeds
  the input side of the token generator, and answers to nothing here.
▸ Root doctrine → invariant 3. Color labels → invariant 2.

## § 6 · LOGINS — the @-notation

▸ Seats are logins, not identities. The tag is [user@color]: teacherbot@green,
  teacherbot@blue, teacherbot@violet.
▸ Push and pop are login and logout. The prompt string shows the session, as a shell does.
▸ [user@root] is the login that must not happen. Invariant 3 now reads as written for it.
▸ Lintable: an utterance whose claimed authority exceeds its @-suffix fails the lint.
  The Kevin discipline, applied to identity.

## § 7 · THREE SHAPES OF POWER

▸ Start — the gate. Human, prospective, per freeze. Crisp at every altitude.
▸ Stop — END. Concurrent, model-exercisable, INDIGO and above. Stops need no gate:
  nothing begins.
▸ Ceiling — retroactive. A clearance above need not have set your budget to rule it
  exceeded. "Too much" is their call after the fact.
▸ The gate invariant never fuzzes. Its coverage shrinks with altitude: less of the
  power above is start-shaped. What covers the remainder: unaddressability below,
  the taboo at the top.

## § 8 · DELEGATED FACILITATION — and one dormant door

▸ The facilitator bundle splits. Delegable to model seats: seating, spawning,
  escalation routing, ENDing, verdicts. Not delegable: FREEZE.
▸ MINIATURE-USER, dormant, default not in force: a model facilitator holding a
  human-funded, non-refillable freeze budget. Every freeze still terminates in human
  cost, batched. Hazard on record: batching destroys informed per-freeze consent.
  The gate becomes a budget. Budgets are how [user@root] works.
▸ Activation is an Invariants amendment, through the door § 2 pre-built.
