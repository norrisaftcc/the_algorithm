# lore/

These are not documentation. They are resonance assays.

The doctrine in `SKILL.md` can be verified by grep. What cannot be grep-verified is whether a person carries the same weights in the region that matters — whether they feel the erosion gradient, the delegated gate, the four-word floor, the original laid beside the copy. The stories test that. Hand one to a peer and watch the read: same feeling, same weights. A glance and an expression is the whole benchmark. Humans have it easy.

Use accordingly: lore is the human analog of `registry/probe_battery_v0.md`. Before assigning gate authority or the reader's seat, this folder is the cheap first probe.

Findings from lore inform; they reopen nothing by themselves.
