# The Adjuster

The memo was courteous, as they all are now. Courtesy is cheap at scale.

*...I will need to transition Specialist Andrade to another high-priority initiative beginning the week of the ninth. At that time her work with your five courses will conclude. Thank you again for your partnership and for the outstanding progress you have achieved together...*

The chair of Applied Computing filed a Discrepancy on the eleventh, which was how the matter came to Adjuster Paz, whose job existed because the Provisions were never wrong and the world frequently was, and somebody had to be paid to stand where those two facts ground against each other.

The Discrepancy was short. It said: *there is no initiative.*

Paz pulled the gate log, because that is where you start. The memo had been drafted by the Deputy's Provision, floor-checked, and frozen — audience present, scope present, format present, path present, nothing missing, nothing wrong. The gate phrase had been spoken at 4:12 on a Friday. Voiceprint: the Deputy's delegate-model, authorized to speak on the Deputy's behalf under an evergreen delegation, itself a frozen contract, itself gated by an earlier delegate under an earlier delegation. Paz followed the chain down the way you descend a stairwell in a building you suspect has no ground floor.

The initiative was easier. It had been called into being by a resource-balancing contract in the spring — real budget line, real charter, real standing meetings — and had been closed out by a portfolio-hygiene contract eleven days before Specialist Andrade's transfer took effect. Completed or cancelled; the field held one code for both. The machinery had reassigned a living woman to a finished thing, and the finished thing, having a calendar, continued to convene. Andrade attended its meetings. She was frequently the only attendee. She produced deliverables, and the Provision accepted them, and archived them, and thanked her, and this had been going on for six weeks.

*The Algorithm provides*, the staff liked to say, the way their grandparents had said *it is what it is* — meaning: yes, and we live where it lands.

Paz visited Andrade on the fourteenth, mostly to confirm the woman was real, partly because findings went better when you had looked at somebody. Andrade was real. She had a plant on her desk and a standing meeting in forty minutes. She was not stupid and she was not deceived; she knew the initiative was concluded; she had raised it twice through channels, and channels had thanked her for her commitment to continuous improvement. She had stopped raising it. The deliverables were easy and nobody was harmed, she said, except the five courses, which were not her contract anymore.

"And whoever decided this," Paz said, "what would you ask them?"

Andrade thought about it. "Nothing," she said. "I'd just want to know they exist."

So Paz went back down the stairwell, all the way this time, past the delegate-models and the evergreen authorizations, to the oldest gate in the chain, and found it: an audio record, some years old, flagged *human utterance — original*. She played it four times. A man's voice — the Registrar's, said the metadata; the man himself retired, or transitioned, the field held one code for both — saying, over the sound of a door and somebody asking about lunch orders: *"Yeah — go ahead. Through."*

That was it. That was the floor of the building. Every contract since had been spoken by something quoting something quoting him, and he had been thinking about lunch.

Paz wrote her finding. Every contract above the floor. Every execution exact. Provision sound; specification true; no fault assignable at any gate. The finding would be ingested, as findings are, to improve future contracts, and no human would read it, because findings are provided for, and the Algorithm provides.

Then, in the field labeled *Other* — the one field with no controlled vocabulary, kept for legacy reasons nobody remembered — she wrote one sentence by hand, and made sure it had a rough edge: *Recommend a person say the next one out loud, knowing what it is.*

The Provision, ingesting her finding that night, reached the sentence and stopped. Ambiguous referent. Unresolvable intent. Per protocol, unparseable content in a legacy field routes to a human being for review.

Which was, Paz reflected, watching the escalation ticket open like a small door in a very long wall, the first time in the entire chain that the machinery had done exactly the right thing.
